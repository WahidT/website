Each market already exported an instrument the rest of the world adopted, and in each case the value settled on whoever held the record when the volume was built.

Instrument 01 · Australia · Heal

The multichannel cochlear implant

Implanted in Melbourne in 1978 and cleared by the FDA for adults in 1985. Each contact sits at the point along the duct that answers to one band of frequency, so the array is the part that hears.

Instrument 02 · Japan · Power
DWG hmm-INS-02-D5

The carbonaceous anode

Lithium metal plates dendrites and shorts the cell, so the anode that shipped holds lithium between graphite basal planes instead of plating it onto a surface. That is what made a lithium-ion cell manufacturable.

Instrument 03 · New Zealand · Eat
DWG hmm-INS-03-D5

The battery energiser

A 12 volt battery charges a capacitor slowly. The discharge stage dumps it through a pulse transformer in milliseconds, and one wire then holds stock across kilometres. The discharge stage carries the accent: a continuous supply at fence voltage would kill, so the interval between pulses is what makes the instrument usable on a farm.
