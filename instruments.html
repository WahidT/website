Instrument 01 · Australia · Heal
DWG hmm-INS-01-D5

The multichannel cochlear implant

Implanted in Melbourne in 1978 and cleared by the FDA for adults in 1985. The accent layer is the electrode array: each contact sits at the point along the duct that answers to one band of frequency, so the array is the part that hears.

Instrument 02 · Japan · Power
DWG hmm-INS-02-D5

The carbonaceous anode

Lithium metal plates dendrites and shorts the cell, so the anode that shipped holds lithium between graphite basal planes instead of plating it onto a surface. That is what made a lithium-ion cell manufacturable, and it is the accent layer here: the anode in the wound roll, resolved in the stack below it.

Instrument 03 · New Zealand · Eat
DWG hmm-INS-03-D5

The battery energiser

A 12 volt battery charges a capacitor slowly, the discharge stage dumps it through a pulse transformer in milliseconds, and one wire then holds stock across kilometres. The accent layer is that discharge stage: a continuous supply at fence voltage would kill, so the interval between pulses is what makes the instrument usable on a farm.
