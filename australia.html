Australia

Power · Eat · Heal.

Australia leads the three markets in health science, and nine in ten of its venture-backed acquisitions go to an operating company. We check the approval each company needs before we invest.

01 · Entry and exit

Australian health has the second-strongest exit-to-entry ratio of the nine cells we model, read as the exit ceiling divided by entry multiple. Power and Eat sit below parity at 0.71x, so we build the Australian book on health.

02 · What Australia produces

1.66Heal

Australia leads in health. Its specialisation index is 1.66 on innovation and 1.12 on exits, the only one of the three markets to lead Heal. Melbourne implanted the first multichannel cochlear implant to reach patients at scale, in 1978.

03 · How we pick

58%of dollars

Australian venture dollars rose 31% in 2025, and the 20 largest rounds took 58% of the money, up from 50%. Capital is concentrating above seed, so the job at seed is picking the companies that will raise the next round.

Cut Through Venture and Folklore Ventures, State of Australian Startup Funding 2025, pp. 12 and 21.

60days in the UK

The United Kingdom's medicines regulator names the Australian regulator one of seven reference regulators under its International Recognition Procedure, introduced in January 2024. An Australian medicine approval can be recognised there on a 60-day timetable, against 210 days for a national assessment. One device audit serves Japan, Australia, the United States, Canada and Brazil, so an Australian approval travels.

MHRA, International Recognition Procedure, updated 9 October 2026; US FDA, Medical Device Single Audit Program.
