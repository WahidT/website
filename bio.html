The three markets were selected on the evidence, and the screen excluded as well as included: Singapore was examined and left out, not yet additive. What his background adds is the ability to work the three that remained. He grew up between New Zealand, Australia and Saudi Arabia, and his working life and friendships have run across Asia since, Japan included.

hmm's method is the work he has already done: reach technical founders before the first round, then build the commercial side with them. The record below is dated and checkable.

  1. GoodFit VP of Revenue · 2022 to present · Global; UK, Europe, United States

    Data platform built to identify companies before they enter a sales pipeline. That is the same problem as reaching a founder before the first round. Leads marketing, sales and post-sale as one organisation, from early revenue through Series A and into Series B readiness.

  2. Skalata Ventures Investment Manager · 2021 to 2022 · Australia, national

    Led the pivot from accelerator to a national pre-seed and seed fund. Built the sourcing engine from nothing and hired the associate team that ran it. That engine reached and closed 15 investments in a year. What it got right, and where it fell short, is what hmm's sourcing is built on now.

  3. Paddle Commercial lead, then Head of APAC · 2017 to 2021 · Global, then APAC and China

    Payments infrastructure for software companies, operating as merchant of record. That structure puts the regulatory burden on the company rather than its customers: tax liability, payments licensing and compliance across every jurisdiction it sells into. The regulated position was the product, which is the mechanism hmm now underwrites from the outside. Built the commercial function from zero as a player-coach for the first three years, then took charge of APAC expansion, focused on China.
